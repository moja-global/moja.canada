Detailed Description

The CBMSpinupDisturbanceModule module applies the transfers of carbon between pools defined by the disturbance matrices belonging to the historic and last pass disturbances in the MAKELIST spin-up procedure.

The MAKELIST spin-up disturbance module does not reset the stand age to 0 if the live biomass is reduced to zero.
