Detailed Description

This module applies and tracks growth multipliers following disturbance events. Growth multipliers modify the pixel’s carbon increments by a proportion that can vary over time to represent degradation or enhancement of the stand. Growth multipliers are normally stored in the growth_multiplier_* tables in the GCBM input database
